# Testing Playbook

## Commands
- `npm run test` – executes Vitest (placeholder suite until systems are implemented).
- `npm run lint` – runs ESLint across all TypeScript modules.
- `npm run build` – ensures the Vite bundle compiles without type errors.

## Manual Smoke
1. `npm run dev` to start the development server.
2. Move with **WASD** and confirm the camera-centred rendering updates the HUD coordinates.
3. Continue travelling east/west for ~10 chunks; watch the tile palette shift and ensure there is no visible hitching as chunks stream in/out. The FPS overlay should remain stable and memory usage should not explode as old chunks unload.
4. While travelling, note the biome toast in the HUD meta line (urban → suburban → highway, etc.) updates alongside the background palette to confirm the new JSON biome definitions are being sampled.
5. Hold **Shift** to sprint, then tap **Ctrl** to crouch. The new noise meter (bottom-left) should spike into yellow/red while sprinting and drop into green when crouched; the light indicator should swap icons as the simulated day/night cycle progresses.
6. Press **Z** to cycle through stealth tools and **X** to deploy one – the tooltip should update with the selected tool/cooldown and zombies should path toward the emitted noise ring.
7. Observe zombie circles change glyph/colour as they hear player footsteps or distraction tools (step around to emit noise rings).
8. Inspect the map canvas as you walk through multiple biomes: POI rectangles should render with category colours/tooltips, and spline-like road lines should appear between major POIs. Move far away, then backtrack to confirm the same POIs/roads respawn deterministically.
9. Press **Tab** to open/close the inventory overlay; confirm multi-cell items span the expected grid footprint, rotation badges appear, and the header weight readout updates when items are added/removed via `window.game.player.inventory.add(...)`.
10. Tap **C** to open the crafting planner. Queue the `Craft Bandage` recipe – the player inventory should lose two cloth scraps and gain a bandage after ~10 seconds. Triggering a recipe without enough materials should surface an inline error message.
11. Press **B** to open the base building planner. Select the diesel generator, hover near the player to preview the ghost outline, and left-click to place – the UI should deduct metal/circuit materials and the power summary should increase available kW. Place a powered gate next to it and observe the outline turn red if you try overlapping structures. Toggle **B** again to hide the planner.
12. Follow the POI overlays (white outlines) until you enter a labelled site (Apartment Block, Strip Mall, Checkpoint, etc.). As you cross into the rectangle a loot scene spawns: look for teal overlays positioned according to the POI template. Approach one, verify the transparent container HUD appears with the **E – Loot All** hint, and empty it. Remain in the area for ~2 minutes to confirm the container respawns using the POI’s timer even if you leave and return.
13. Approach the red semi tractor south of spawn. When the vehicle hint prompts **E – Enter**, press **E** to take the driver seat. Use **W/S** to accelerate/brake, **A/D** to steer, and confirm the player avatar locks to the cab as it moves. Press **V** to open the transparent trailer cargo HUD, verify the grid reflects the stored sedan/crates with a live kg readout, then press **V** again or **E** to exit the vehicle – the hint should update accordingly.
14. Open a POI container, loot a few stacks into the backpack, then press **F5**. Walk 30+ tiles away so the container despawns, press **F9**, and return to the same POI – the container should still be empty (until its respawn timer completes) and the player inventory/position should match the moment you saved.
15. Press **J** to open the survivor roster. Change Mateos's assignment to Mechanic, trigger the "Mission Success" morale button, and confirm morale/relationship numbers update while the roster list reflects the new job label. Close the panel with the × button or **J** again.
16. Hit **R** to open the raid planner. Observe faction standings, wait ~30 seconds for a convoy intel window, then click **Ambush** – the status banner should confirm success, reputation drops for that faction, and the intel feed log captures the loot summary.
17. Watch the top-right progression card + HUD meta line. Walk 200+ units away from the origin to push into ring 2 and confirm the readouts update (ring number, loot tiers, spawn target). Keep moving to ring 3 and note the zombie mix shifts toward runners.
18. Loot both starter crates to raise backpack weight past 30kg and place a powered gate to raise defense. The base-heat row should jump above 30% and surface a yellow warning when a siege threshold is crossed; note the "Next Siege" percentage decreases as heat climbs.
19. Let the server run for ~3 minutes (roughly half a day). Confirm the season row rotates (e.g., Spring → Summer) and the effects list updates; the HUD meta line should echo the same season/effect text.
20. Firearms: ensure the pistol is selected (weapon HUD line shows "Service Pistol"), aim with the mouse, and hold **Mouse1**. Each shot should spawn a yellow tracer, decrement the magazine counter, and trigger a red noise ring that zombies path toward.
21. Reload + reserve tracking: after emptying the pistol magazine press **F** – the weapon HUD should display a reload percentage, the magazine refills using 9mm ammo from the backpack, and reserve ammo drops by the same amount. Attempting to reload with no ammo should surface a HUD message.
22. Attachments + disassembly: press **T** to open the weapon mod panel while the AK-47 is selected. Install the Reflex Sight on the optic slot, close the panel, and notice the HUD ammo line shows the new spread/range bonus (and shots feel tighter). Reopen the panel, detach the optic (it should return to the backpack), then click **Disassemble** to break the weapon into metal/circuit parts even though it was equipped.
23. Grenades: ensure a grenade type is available in the backpack. Press **G** to cycle through Frag/Molotov/Flashbang and watch the HUD weapon line append the current selection. Tap **g** to throw toward the cursor – the grenade should arc forward, explode after its fuse, damage any nearby zombies, emit explosion noise, and decrement the grenade count in the HUD tooltip.
24. Zombie damage + healing: allow a zombie to reach melee range; note the vitals card flashes red, HP drops, and bleed/infection statuses appear. Press **H** to consume a medkit/bandage – health should jump, bleed clears (medkit) and the HUD message confirms which item was used. Let HP fall to zero to confirm the player becomes “Downed” and movement locks until you heal or reload.

Future QA scenarios from the mega-spec will be added as systems come online.
